This is the Engines and Wagons component of JP+. 

Github link: https://github.com/OpenTTD-JPplus/JPengines

### Overview

JP+ Engines & Wagons features a wide variety of Japanese locomotives and rolling stock. It focuses exclusively on locomotive-hauled trains, as multiple units are featured in other JP+ components. 

JP+ Engines begins in 1872, at the dawn of railways in Japan. This gives it the earliest possible starting date out of all JP+ components.

All vehicles in JP+ Engines use narrow-gauge railtypes if available. This is because the vast majority of Japan's rail network is narrow-gauge, with the exception of Shinkansen and some private passenger railways, which are featured in other JP+ components.

JP+ Engines re-uses many graphics from [[JapanSet]], often with improvements and modifications. Due to the variety of different artists, sprites may not always be consistent but work is slowly progressing to improve them.

### Features

* Steam Engines 
* Diesel Engines
* Electric Engines
* Passenger Coaches
* Freight Wagons

* Wide variety of liveries and variants, including JGR, JNR, JR as well as private companies and experimental liveries
* Full refit compatibility with industry sets such as FIRS and AXIS, including custom cargo graphics where available
* All freight wagons support autorefit (refit at stations)
* Brake van speed boost feature - brake vans will cause most freight wagons to increase their top speed to match the brake van, allowing to mix different freight wagons without compromising the top speed
* Both automatic and custom formations for passenger trains
